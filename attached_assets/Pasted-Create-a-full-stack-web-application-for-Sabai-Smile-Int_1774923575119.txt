Create a full-stack web application for "Sabai Smile International," a Thailand-based wellness and export company. The application must include ecommerce functionality, a B2B distributor portal, multi-payment gateway integration, and content management for six core service areas.

## PROJECT OVERVIEW

Sabai Smile International is a holistic wellness company offering:
1. Wellness Export Products (Thai herbal compresses, massage oils, organic supplements, spa equipment)
2. Traditional Thai Massage Promotion (certification courses, therapist booking)
3. Medical Devices Export (B2B medical equipment)
4. Medical Health Tourism (fertility treatments, IVF packages)
5. Environmental Sustainability (eco-products + eco-tourism training programs)
6. Business Skills & English Training (corporate training for Thai businesses)
7. Tour Thailand Packages (curated travel itineraries)

The platform must serve both B2C retail customers and B2B international distributors with tiered pricing and wholesale ordering.

## TECH STACK REQUIREMENTS

- **Backend:** Node.js with Express.js or Python with Django/Flask
- **Frontend:** React.js or Vue.js with responsive design (mobile-first)
- **Database:** PostgreSQL (for relational data: users, orders, products) + MongoDB (for content pages, blog)
- **Authentication:** JWT with role-based access (Admin, Distributor, Agency Partner, Retail Customer)
- **File Storage:** Cloud storage (AWS S3 or similar) for product images and marketing assets
- **Payment Integration:** Stripe API, PayPal API, PromptPay (Thai QR code) integration
- **Email:** Nodemailer or SendGrid for order confirmations and partner notifications
- **Hosting:** Configured for deployment on Replit or exportable to Vercel/Railway

### Wire Transfer & Letter of Credit

- Display bank account details for wire transfer
- Mark order as pending until payment confirmation

## SHIPPING & LOGISTICS INTEGRATION

### Shipping Methods
- **Air Freight:** For urgent, small-volume orders
- **Sea Freight (LCL):** For medium-volume orders
- **Sea Freight (FCL):** For full container loads
- **Express Courier:** DHL/FedEx for samples and small packages

### Shipping Logic
- Calculate shipping cost based on:
  - Total weight of items
  - Destination country
  - Selected shipping method
- Integrate with a shipping API (e.g., Shippo, EasyShip) or use pre-configured rates
- For sea freight, provide estimated cost based on volume (CBM) with a note that final quote will be provided

### Documentation Generation
- Auto-generate Commercial Invoice (PDF) with:
  - Shipper details (Sabai Smile)
  - Consignee details (distributor)
  - Product descriptions, HS codes, quantities, unit values
  - Total value, freight terms (FOB Bangkok)
- Auto-generate Packing List (PDF)
- Generate Certificate of Origin for ASEAN shipments

## BULK ORDER SYSTEM

For distributors, implement CSV bulk upload:
1. Template download with SKU and quantity columns
2. Upload validation:
   - Check SKU exists
   - Check quantity meets MOQ
   - Check stock availability
3. Display validation errors with line numbers

## REFERRAL SYSTEM

For agency partners:
1. Generate unique referral link (e.g., sabaismile.com/?ref=AGENCY123)

4. When referred user makes first purchase, calculate commission (percentage configurable in settings)
5. Mark commission as pending, then paid when order is completed
