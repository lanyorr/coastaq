**4.1 Full Refund**

**4.2 Partial Refund**

- Item not as described but you choose to keep it
- Slight damage or wear not mentioned
- Delayed shipping

**4.3 No Refund**

- Use tracked shipping for returns

1. Buyer ships item back (5-10 days)
2. Seller receives and inspects (3-5 days)
3. Seller issues refund (1-3 days)
